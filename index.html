Procedures

Monitoring SLA
  • High Volume recorded IVR message is on during all Peaks.
  • After the first Five9 report email, the assigned Director and Associate/Assistant Director (per the schedule) must run the Five9 "OFS Queue Performance (Today by Hour)" report manually every 30 minutes — or more often — to verify SLA improvement.
Steps to notify the team
  • Directors notify their ADs via Teams chat.
  • Assistant Directors notify their FSAs via Teams chat.
  • Associate Directors notify their Seniors, Nesters & Trainees via Teams chat.
  • Notify FSAs and Seniors of improved color status when the +5% buffer is met.
  • Escalation: Assigned Director & Associate/Assistant Director → back-up 1: Unassigned Director → back-up 2: AVP posts the update to the OFS Leadership chat and the High Volume Updates channel.
